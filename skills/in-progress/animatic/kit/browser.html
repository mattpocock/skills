Rate limits

Every API key gets 100 requests a minute. Go over it and you get a 429 until the minute is up.

Reading your remaining budget

Each response carries X-RateLimit-Remaining, so a client can slow down before it is blocked.

GET /orders X-API-Key: ak_live_3f9… X-RateLimit-Remaining: 41
